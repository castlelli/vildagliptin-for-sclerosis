# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static, i18n routing: `/` = EN, `/pt/` = PT), D3 v7 charts as vanilla-JS islands, deployed to GitHub Pages via Actions at `castlelli.github.io/vildagliptin-for-sclerosis`. Chosen as "delegated" after the user approved installing tooling; mirrors the stack of the parent portfolio (`castlelli.github.io`, Astro).

## Users

- Primary: people evaluating the author's portfolio — recruiters, PIs, biotech/pharma founders and hiring managers — who arrive from castlelli.github.io and decide within a minute whether the author can do rigorous computational biology and communicate it.
- Secondary: scientists curious about the repurposing hypothesis who want the evidence, numbers and references.

## Product Purpose

A project page for a seven-day drug-repositioning hackathon (Cortellis Drug Repositioning Hackathon, pitched Aug 31, 2026): should vildagliptin (DPP-4 inhibitor, approved for type 2 diabetes) be tested against skin fibrosis in systemic sclerosis? Success = a visitor understands the question, the evidence chain, the honest limits and the decision, and leaves convinced of the author's rigor.

## Positioning

The project's decision is "advance to a disease-relevant proof-of-mechanism experiment — not a clinical efficacy claim". It reports results that refined or contradicted its own model (DPP4 not globally increased, not a severity marker, no linear trajectory, stress tests added no support) as openly as the supporting ones.

## Operating Context

Read on desktop and mobile, often shared as a link. Bilingual EN/PT. Part of the author's personal portfolio; a work card on castlelli.github.io links here.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Team project (five people). Shown without names: "built with a five-person hackathon team; I co-led the computational analysis and pitched the case." No AI co-authoring credits.
- Outcome claims limited to the deck: no award, no follow-up lab work. Prospects = the designed experiment and open questions (skin exposure, target engagement).
- Exact numbers available only for: network proximity z/q, mRSS ρ, Kpu thresholds, IC50s. Other chart values are digitized from deck figures and must be labeled approximate.
- Upstream analysis code no longer exists; the notebook only renders frozen results.

## Brand Commitments

User-pinned identity: "Pharma startup + MIT/Harvard PhD — scientific, but new and creative." No AI slop, no clichés. User-pinned direction: modern lab notebook (warm paper, ink serif, one fluorescent tracer accent, margin citations).

## Evidence on Hand

- Gamma deck "Hackathon - Grupo D" (all text, figures, ~31 references).
- `vildagliptin_ssc_figures.py` (hardcoded values, figure logic).
- Deck images (own notebook figures + third-party: Distler 2026 schematic, Soare 2020 figure, Cortellis screenshot — used with full credit).
- Absent, must not be fabricated: clinical data, awards, follow-up results, measured skin PK.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim carries a Nature-style citation; every number names its source.
2. Show the refusals as prominently as the support.
3. The reader can touch the evidence: charts are interactive, not decorative.
4. Hypothesis, never treatment advice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA; reduced-motion respected; charts have text equivalents; both languages complete.
